Razorback Bowling Tryouts

Interested in competing for the University of Arkansas Bowling Club? Tryouts are open to University of Arkansas students who want to join the team, compete in tournaments, and be part of collegiate bowling at Arkansas.

2026-2027 Tryouts: August 13-16

Attendance is required each day of tryouts. Each day will include different oil patterns, formats, and evaluations, so bowlers must attend all tryout sessions to be fully considered. Please mention in your email if you will have trouble attending a specific day, and we may be able to help.

Who Can Try Out?

Any University of Arkansas student interested in competitive bowling is welcome to try out. Bowlers of different experience levels are encouraged to attend.

What to Bring

Bring your bowling equipment, including bowling balls, shoes, and any other gear. Bring as much as you can, as you will be bowling on multiple sport shots.

What to Expect

Tryouts will take place over multiple days and each day will feature different oil patterns, formats, and evaluation areas. Bowlers will be evaluated on shot making, spare shooting, consistency, attitude, teamwork, and overall fit for the program.

Questions?

If you have questions about tryouts, eligibility, or the team, please email Coach Ezell. He will be happy to help with any questions or concerns.
